# Dairy farm launch decision · January 2027

**Outcome:** Decide whether to proceed with a phase-one 20-animal dairy setup and a safe, planned path to 40. January 2027 is conditional on site, species, permissions, cash flow, veterinary and procurement gates.

## Gates before final bids

- Confirm site/village, land rights, access, flood/drainage constraints, setbacks, soil and local requirements.
- Confirm cow, buffalo or mixed herd; production model, veterinary plan, feed/fodder, water, manure handling and milk buyer route.
- Before buying animals, have the local veterinarian define sourcing criteria, health checks and records, isolation/acclimation, transport, and a phased purchase plan for the confirmed species and 20-head launch.
- Have a qualified local veterinarian and engineer review housing, ventilation, flooring, drainage, waste handling and expansion.
- Survey electrical loads before solar sizing: pumps, milking/chilling equipment, lighting, CCTV, communications and backup. Before comparing a grid-connected design, confirm the premises' consumer category and sanctioned load, permitted grid-solar/net-metering setup, required upgrades and utility approvals with APEPDCL or a qualified electrical professional. Check subsidy eligibility separately. If solar CCTV only is considered, document that distinct electrical and safety scope.
- Prepare phased capital and operating budgets, contingency, working capital and a downside cash-flow case. Include unit economics and production continuity: verified milk buyer/offtake terms, feed and labor costs, species-specific yield assumptions, lactation/dry-period coverage, veterinary reserves, staggered sourcing, downtime and the cash required before stable production. Model 20 animals first and the path to 40 separately; do not treat the 40-animal expansion as committed capacity.

## Comparable supplier scopes

1. **Shed constructor:** site visit, drawings, quantities, drainage/ventilation, animal-welfare fit, expansion interface, inclusions/exclusions, schedule, warranty, milestones and defect correction.
2. **Solar installer:** load survey, grid/off-grid/hybrid rationale, generation assumptions, equipment model, structure, protection, battery where justified, approvals, commissioning, monitoring, warranty and service response.
3. **CCTV installer:** coverage map, night performance, storage/retention, network/power backup, privacy, remote access controls, support and itemized cost.

Use one request pack and compare bids like for like. Verify legal identity, applicable registrations, insurance, relevant references, site visit and service. Have qualified professionals review the scope. Directory listings are leads, not endorsements. Do not appoint solely on ratings or the word “best”.

The current preliminary vendor list, public source links, quote questions and weighted comparison scorecard are in [supplier shortlist and verification plan](supplier-shortlist.md). Those are research leads only; no supplier has been contacted or verified.
