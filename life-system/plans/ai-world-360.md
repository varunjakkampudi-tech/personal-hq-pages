# AI World 360 · daily publishing

## Daily pipeline

1. Prepare for the requested target cadence of two original videos daily, at 12:00 and 19:00 IST. Choose an original topic for each video and validate interest with vidIQ. Record sources for factual claims.
2. Draft the hook, script and storyboard with ChatGPT; use vidIQ for topic/keyword research and metadata analysis. Compare title, description and tag suggestions with channel fit, sources and the approved brief; fact-check claims and originality.
3. Generate visuals with Google Flow. Keep source media in approved private storage and verify third-party asset rights.
4. Edit captions, audio and pacing; review each platform export and thumbnail/cover.
5. Prepare YouTube and Instagram metadata and check current platform requirements.
6. Have Varun review the exact final asset, caption, thumbnail and destination before upload or schedule activation.
7. Verify both platforms separately. Record live URLs and publish times; a draft or schedule is not completion.
8. Check 24-hour and 7-day analytics when available. Carry forward any failed platform leg.

Create two dated records per day when working toward the requested 12:00 and 19:00 IST target, one per video, with topic, sources, script version, generation/edit references, rights review, approval, status, live URLs, analytics and learning. The cadence is an owner target, not a claim that the workflow is currently operating. Do not overwrite earlier records or put credentials/OTPs in the tracker. The format, niche, language and production capacity still need confirmation. The requested target times are 12:00 and 19:00 IST; exact assets and metadata remain subject to the release approval gate. This plan does not approve a specific post.

## Daily run card

Create one copy of `life-system/templates/ai-world-360-daily-record.md` for each video, with two records per day when following the requested 12:00 and 19:00 IST target. Keep source files and private approvals in approved private storage; the public repository can contain only non-sensitive completion state and links already public.

1. **Brief:** record intended audience, one-sentence takeaway, language, format, duration target, platform destinations and the approved channel voice. If any are unknown, mark `Needs owner input`; do not fill with guesses.
2. **Research:** save the vidIQ query/result date and primary sources for factual claims. Separate verified fact, creative framing and opinion. Drop claims that cannot be verified.
3. **Script:** obtain the channel's saved voice guidance before drafting YouTube hooks/scripts. Record script version and fact-check status. Run an independent clarity, story-logic, sensitivity and originality review; log fixes and open questions.
4. **Narration timing:** read the exact script aloud at intended delivery speed, record measured duration and revise to fit. Estimates are not measurements.
5. **Storyboard and generation:** map every shot to narration, prompt, duration and asset source. Generate only after the script/storyboard are owner-reviewed; record Flow job/output references and retain originals privately.
6. **Rights and safety:** account for each image, video, music, voice, likeness, logo and template. Record original creation or licence/permission evidence and scope. A disclaimer or AI-generated label does not establish permission. Any unresolved item is a release blocker.
7. **Edit and accessibility:** verify captions word-for-word against narration, readable mobile framing, contrast, audio mix, safe margins, pacing and platform-specific export. Inspect the actual final files, not only a storyboard or thumbnail.
8. **Packaging:** prepare title, description/caption, thumbnail/cover, hashtags and destination-specific crop. Check every factual statement and link. Record exact versions.
9. **Approval gate:** show Varun the exact final video files, on-screen text, audio, captions, thumbnail, copy, destinations and schedule. Record the exact approved version/hash and approval timestamp. Silence, an earlier general workflow request or a saved draft is not approval.
10. **Publish and reconcile:** publish/schedule only the approved version. Record each platform leg independently as draft, scheduled, submitted, completed, failed or cancelled. Confirm live URL and time per destination; do not count a draft or scheduled item as published.
11. **Learn:** at about 24 hours and 7 days (when analytics are available), record views/reach, retention/watch time, engagement and one learning with the source/date. Avoid comparing unlike formats without noting the difference. Failed or unverified legs stay open with a next action.

### Release states

- `Blocked`: a required source, rights, asset, factual check or technical export check is unresolved.
- `Needs owner input`: voice, audience, goal, exact text, final asset, destination or schedule needs the owner's decision.
- `Ready for exact review`: all production checks are documented; nothing has been published or scheduled.
- `Approved`: exact asset and all destination values were explicitly approved; this alone does not mean publication.
- `Scheduled`: platform confirms a schedule; this alone does not mean publication.
- `Published`: destination reports completion and a public URL/time has been verified.
- `Reviewed`: 24-hour and 7-day checks are recorded or explicitly unavailable/not yet due.

Do not schedule a release while any safety, rights, content-accuracy, final-asset or exact-approval gate is open. Keep owner-only facts and source records private. This run card is a process guide, not approval for any current draft or post.
