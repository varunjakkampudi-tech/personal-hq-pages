# Weekly routine, gym and food

This is general starter guidance, not a personalized medical or nutrition plan. Before tailoring, capture goals, experience, schedule, sleep, equipment, food pattern, allergies, health constraints and clinician guidance. Do not assume calories, weight targets or supplements.

| Day | Movement | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | Comfortable walk, 15–20 min | Choose one work priority |
| Tue | Easy full-body strength, 15–20 min | Leave effort in reserve |
| Wed | Comfortable walk, 15–20 min | Career or project step |
| Thu | Rest or gentle mobility | Recovery and sleep |
| Fri | Easy strength, 15–20 min | Close one open item |
| Sat | Comfortable walk, 20–25 min | AI World 360 production block |
| Sun | Rest | Weekly review and plan |

If currently inactive, start shorter and build gradually. WHO adult guidance is 150–300 minutes of moderate activity and muscle strengthening on at least two days weekly; build toward it according to ability and recovery. Stop for chest pain, faintness, unusual breathlessness or sharp pain and seek appropriate care.

Build varied meals from vegetables/fruit, pulses or beans, whole grains/millets and suitable protein (eggs, fish, chicken, paneer, tofu or curd based on preference). Adapt portions and timing to appetite, allergies, health needs and budget. Avoid restrictive changes until needs are known.

Track movement minutes, strength sessions, sleep window, energy and symptoms. Adjust one thing at a time. Seek a clinician or dietitian for medical conditions, injuries or major diet changes. Sources: [WHO physical activity](https://www.who.int/initiatives/behealthy/physical-activity); [ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024](https://nin.res.in/dietaryguidelines/pdfjs/locale/DGI_2024.pdf).
