# Career transition · February 2027

**Outcome:** By 31 January 2027, prepare a verified master resume, approved role direction, reviewed profiles and a repeatable search routine for a February 2027 company switch. February is a target, not a promise of an offer or start date.

## Sequence

1. **Intake and evidence (by 30 Nov 2026):** privately gather the current resume, LinkedIn/Naukri/Indeed/Cutshort profile links, role and location preferences, notice period, compensation constraints, and evidence for employment dates and outcomes. Keep resume contents and private contacts out of this public repository.
2. **Role direction (by 6 Dec):** choose one primary and up to two adjacent role families; map skills and gaps. Build an achievement bank with evidence for each claim.
3. **Master resume (by 18 Dec):** prepare an ATS-readable source resume and justified variants. Verify dates, titles, responsibilities, tools and metrics. Review before using it.
4. **LinkedIn and Naukri (by 5 Jan):** update headline, summary, chronology, skills, projects and preferences from the approved source. Check visibility and current account settings.
5. **Indeed and Cutshort (by 10 Jan):** refresh or create only after checking onboarding, imported data, visibility and account-link permissions. Inspect each field before saving.
6. **Additional channels (by 12 Jan):** compare platforms such as Instahyre, Wellfound, Foundit, Hirist or company career pages. Treat these as candidates: verify role fit, location, current operation, privacy and cost before registering.
7. **Readiness (by 31 Jan):** finish interview stories, portfolio evidence, networking cadence and an application tracker. Review each profile and outreach message before submission.

## Profile checklist

| Channel | Review | Gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn | Headline, About, Experience, Skills, Featured, visibility | Resume facts verified; profile visibility reviewed |
| Naukri | Resume, profile completeness, skills, role/location/notice preferences | Resume approved; recruiter visibility reviewed |
| Indeed | CV/profile, preferences, alerts | Current upload and privacy settings checked |
| Cutshort | Role profile and connected/imported account data | Current data sharing and connection terms reviewed |
| Other portals | Prioritize current company career pages; consider [Instahyre](https://help.instahyre.com/en/article/what-is-instahyre-1ufln5/) for technology roles, [Wellfound India](https://wellfound.com/location/india) for startup roles, and [Hirist](https://www.hirist.tech/stack-careers) for technology openings | Role relevance, current operation, data visibility and cost checked |

[Foundit](https://www.foundit.in/) is another general-market option to assess. Its current terms describe use of publicly accessible profile information, including resume and contact details, for job-related visibility and outreach. Review the live privacy controls and terms carefully before enabling discoverability or sharing a resume. Do not create every account by default; use the smallest set that fits the approved role direction.

Every resume statement needs evidence or must be framed as scope, not an unverified result. No account creation, profile save, application or outreach should be submitted without the user's review. Keep credentials and OTPs out of Personal HQ.
