# ValueMela storefront operational readiness

This is an evidence checklist for deciding whether the store can safely accept and fulfill customer orders. It does not establish legal, tax, payment-provider or marketplace compliance; resolve those requirements for the actual business model before launch or paid promotion. Keep customer records, credentials, tax identifiers, private addresses and payment details in approved private systems.

## Evidence gates

- [ ] Product catalog: verify each advertised product is available, accurately described and priced, with a named party responsible for inventory and fulfillment.
- [ ] Seller responsibility: document which legal entity accepts the order, issues the invoice, owns/dispatches stock, handles support and bears each refund or cancellation action.
- [ ] Payment: verify the live customer payment journey, settlement destination, reconciliation owner, failure/refund path and provider account state. Do not assume a payment button proves settlement works.
- [ ] Service area and delivery: confirm destinations that can actually be served, carrier/fulfillment handoff, delivery estimates, shipping charges and exception handling.
- [ ] Customer policies: review current cancellation, returns, refund, damaged/late delivery and support processes; make applicable customer-facing terms clear before checkout.
- [ ] Support operations: test the published customer contact route and assign an owner and response process. Keep account login and first-order alert routing intact.
- [ ] End-to-end test: perform a controlled test order through checkout, confirmation, fulfillment/status updates, cancellation or return, refund and payment reconciliation. Keep test/customer identifiers private.
- [ ] Launch decision: record which gates passed, evidence date, remaining blockers and the owner decision. Do not start paid acquisition while material order, support, payment, tax or fulfillment controls remain unverified.
