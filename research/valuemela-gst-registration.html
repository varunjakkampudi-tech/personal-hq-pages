Conditional e-commerce warehouse route

CGST Rule 14B (Notification 05/2026-Central Tax, generally effective 1 November 2026) may allow a same-PAN regular registration in another State/UT where there is no physical presence, using a consenting section 52 tax-collecting e-commerce operator warehouse as principal place of business. It applies to specified goods supplies through that operator and is subject to a home-State GST registration, operator consent, biometric Aadhaar/document checks and physical verification. The specified monthly output-tax liability for supplies to registered recipients other than the same PAN must not exceed ₹2,50,000; this is a tax-liability test, not a sales/turnover cap. The 18 November 2026 Gazette corrigendum G.S.R. 1001(E) corrects Rule 14B numbering and cross-references. Have a GST practitioner verify the corrected official text and live portal flow against ValueMela facts before considering this conditional route.

ValueMela information to establish

  • Legal entity/constitution, PAN state, principal/additional business premises
  • Who owns and operates the marketplace/site, sells each item, invoices buyers, collects payment, and dispatches goods
  • Own-account vs third-party seller flows, marketplace channels, taxable/exempt product mix, states served, stock locations and turnover estimate
  • Applicable registration basis, state registrations, product codes/tax treatment, e-commerce/TCS, composition eligibility and compliance obligations—confirmed by CA

Application preparation

  1. Confirm the appropriate REG-01 route and entity details.
  2. Use the live official GST Portal checklist for constitution, promoters/partners, authorised signatory, premises possession proof, goods/services, state details and verification method.
  3. Keep actual PAN, Aadhaar, OTP, bank data, private address documents, signed entity records and GST credentials in secure business storage only.
  4. Submit through gst.gov.in after owner review. Track acknowledgement and any clarification request.
  5. After approval, verify the GSTIN/certificate and confirm invoice setup and the filing calendar with the CA.
