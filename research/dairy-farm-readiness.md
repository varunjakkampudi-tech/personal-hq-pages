# Dairy farm readiness plan · January 2027 target

**Scope:** Plan a phase-one dairy farm for 20 animals with land and infrastructure that can expand to 40. **Go-live is conditional:** January is a target, not permission to buy animals, sign a construction contract, or open before site, veterinary, market, compliance and cash-flow gates are satisfied.

**Known planning area:** West Godavari, Andhra Pradesh. **Still unknown:** exact village and plot, land rights/use, species and breed mix, intended milk channel, budget, water and power capacity, staffing, and operator/entity. No plot survey, buyer commitment, authority determination or supplier quote is recorded. Do not size facilities, solar, CCTV, herd feed, or revenue around assumptions that have not been verified.

## Decisions required first

Capture the answers in a private working file; put only completion status in Personal HQ.

- Exact plot, village/mandal, survey/site plan, access, owner/lease and land-use status.
- Cows, buffaloes or a defined mix; breeds, lactating/dry/calving groups and whether 20 means total animals or 20 milking animals.
- Phase-one stocking and a physically feasible route to 40, including young stock, sick isolation and quarantine.
- Milk route: cooperative, chilling centre, processor, direct sale or another verified buyer; quality tests, collection time, price/payment formula and backup outlet.
- Capex ceiling, working-capital reserve, funding sources, owner/approver and maximum acceptable operating loss during ramp-up.
- Available water yield and quality, power connection and outage pattern, network coverage, drainage/flood level, road access and neighbour/environment constraints.

## Workstreams to price and verify

### Site, permissions and infrastructure

- Obtain a boundary/topography/site survey and check flood history, soil bearing, access and drainage with qualified local professionals.
- Ask the local body/revenue authority and relevant state offices which land-use, construction, water extraction, waste, electrical and business permissions apply to this exact plot and activity. Record answers; do not infer that a dairy farm is exempt.
- Design for safe animal and worker flow, shade, ventilation, non-slip surfaces, clean water access, feed storage, milking, calving, sick isolation, quarantine, manure handling and expansion. Have a local structural professional verify calculations and code/permit requirements.
- Test water quantity and quality for animal use and cleaning. NDDB advises continuous access to clean drinking water; actual daily demand must be calculated for the chosen species, production, heat and cleaning plan with a veterinarian/engineer.
- Specify solar from measured daily and critical loads, outage duration, grid condition and pump/milking equipment. Before comparing a grid-connected design, record the premises' consumer category and sanctioned load and obtain written confirmation from APEPDCL or a qualified electrical professional on permissible grid-solar/net-metering configuration, connection upgrades and required utility approvals. Verify subsidy eligibility separately with the administering authority. If considering standalone solar CCTV only, document its separate electrical and safety requirements. Specify CCTV from a camera coverage map, night conditions, retention, network, secure access and outage backup. Do not select by advertised kW or camera count alone.

### Animals, health and operations

- Have a qualified local veterinarian define the herd profile, sourcing checks, quarantine, vaccination, disease response, breeding/calving, mastitis prevention and emergency coverage before selecting animals.
- Verify each animal's identity, age, breed, reproductive/lactation history, health records and seller/ownership documents. Examine animals with a veterinarian before purchase.
- Decide whether insurance is available and appropriate; verify current policy wording, exclusions, identification and veterinary examination requirements with the insurer and veterinarian.
- Create daily records for feed, water, milk yield/quality, health/treatment, breeding/calving, mortality, expenses, sales and staff duties before the first animal arrives.
- Prepare disease isolation, cleaning, visitor/vehicle controls and dead-stock/urgent-care procedures with the veterinarian and local authority as applicable.

### Milk route and compliance

- Confirm buyer acceptance, collection time, volume/quality tests, pricing formula, deductions, rejection process, payment timing, chilling/transport expectations and an alternate outlet in writing where possible.
- Map the actual activity: primary production only, collection/handling, chilling, processing, packing, storage, transport, sale of raw milk, or milk products. Ask the local Food Safety office/FoSCoS which FSSAI registration or licence category applies to the operator and premises; classification and eligibility depend on the activity and current rules.
- Ask a CA/GST practitioner to confirm the operator/entity, GST registration basis, invoice flow and tax obligations for the actual business and milk-sales route. Do not assume the dairy plan has the same GST route as ValueMela.
- Keep compliance applications, credentials, identity records, bank details, signed contracts and veterinary personal data in secure official/private systems. The HQ records milestones, owners and due dates only.

## Quote comparison gate

Use `research/supplier-shortlist-west-godavari.md` as an unverified enquiry list. Request a site visit and a like-for-like, itemised bid that names assumptions and exclusions. Verify business identity and GST registration against the contracting entity; get two relevant references with permission to contact; inspect warranty, service response, insurance, drawings/calculations, payment milestones, defect correction and schedule. Ask a veterinarian and qualified engineer to review the animal and engineering scope. Do not appoint a bidder on directory ratings or the word “best”.
